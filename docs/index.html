Data & methodology

Survey data come from the ECB Survey of Professional Forecasters and Consensus Economics. The term structure is estimated with a Bayesian version of the model of Aruoba (2020). Only model output is published here, not the underlying survey data.

Borağan Aruoba, S. (2020). Term structures of inflation expectations and real interest rates. Journal of Business & Economic Statistics 38(3): 542–553.
